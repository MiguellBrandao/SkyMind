Not another stats card.

Most SkyBlock bots show you a screenshot of your own profile. SkyMind is a real agent — it decides what to look up, fetches it live from Hypixel, and writes the answer itself. Ask a question the way you'd ask a friend who actually plays: "is this worth upgrading," "what am I missing for M6," "how much would this cost right now."

What it does

TIERCommon

/profile  /stats

Level, real Minecraft item icons, skills, dungeon classes, slayers, equipment — for you or anyone by username.

TIERRare

/networth

A real net worth calculation, broken down by armor, accessories, pets, and museum — not a rough guess.

TIEREpic

/ask

A tool-calling agent that reads your live profile, checks current Bazaar and Auction House prices, and searches the SkyBlock Wiki and community discussion before it answers — never a guess dressed up as fact.

TIERLegendary

/link

Proves it's your account using a setting already in your SkyBlock menu. No password, no Microsoft login, no API key of yours, ever.

Built properly, not just fast

No passwords, ever.

Linking works by matching a code against a field already in your Hypixel account settings. SkyMind never asks for a Minecraft, Microsoft, or Discord password, and never asks you for your own Hypixel API key.

Live data, respectfully cached.

Every number comes from Hypixel's Public API at the moment you ask. Responses are cached only briefly — Bazaar for under a minute, auctions for two — to stay well under rate limits, never to build a long-term history of anyone's stats.

Independent, not official.
